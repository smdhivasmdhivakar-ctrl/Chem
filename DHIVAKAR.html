What this workspace does

  • Searches PubChem for compounds and periodic-table elements
  • Shows molecules in 3D with four render modes, labels, surface and spin
  • Reads any SMILES string and calculates formula, weight and bond counts
  • Pulls identity data, structure images, descriptions and experimental properties
  • Adds ChemSpider records when you supply a free API key
